0.9
===
 - 0.9.15.4 The camera scope wears its real housing: live picture behind the
            lens glass, and yaw, pitch and zoom on three LED readouts with a
            hand-made minus digit. Servers shrug off packet spam - camera
            renders and exports are rate limited, hostile packet values
            bounce at decode, and spectators can't press buttons. Sign,
            banner and map caches stop growing forever; maps update on
            camera instead of freezing. Ejecting a tape or breaking a deck
            mid-recording commits the footage first. Recording buffers get
            a hard memory ceiling, and voice decoders, microphones and
            camera assets all clean up when the server stops.
 - 0.9.15.3 Microphone sound table loads in the background, so a server no
            longer freezes at boot waiting on Mojang, and one bad mod jar
            can't mute world sounds forever. Recording fps snaps to rates
            the game can actually deliver, so tape headers tell the truth.
            A VCR demoted mid-recording commits its footage instead of
            losing it. The capture card GUI updates live, on-screen errors
            linger long enough to read, and entity skins obey the same
            single-host rule as player heads.
 - 0.9.15.2 Camera links survive chunk reloads. Two people can talk over
            each other on a microphone and both come through; distant
            voices keep their clarity instead of dissolving into noise.
            Cameras shrug off broken models, strange minecart cargo and
            crafted player heads instead of crashing or fetching from
            attacker-chosen hosts.
 - 0.9.15.1 Hardens the network layer: packet directions pinned, hostile
            size claims and NaN aim rejected. Recordings now commit to tape
            when their chunk unloads instead of vanishing, and tapes keep
            working past their 999th recording. Cassettes drop their flat
            GUI sprites and render as their models everywhere, lit and
            centered like blocks.
 - 0.9.15.0 VCR, camera, intercom and desktop mic remodeled and retextured;
            VCR display reborn on an in-house dot-matrix font. New compact
            cassette item with two-sided anvil labels; VHS reels behind real
            glass; cassette label strips take dye and cauldrons wash it off.
            Intercom gains a programmable screen, the desktop mic's mute
            button physically mutes, and the capture card records computer
            and turtle screens. Cameras now see the mod's own content -
            tapes inside decks, display text - and only report players
            truly in frame.
 - 0.9.14.0 VCR gets a red 12-cell segment display, settable from Lua; mics
            hear the whole world, not just voices, in tiny 16 kHz DFPWM.
 - 0.9.13.0 Capture card records at the monitor's true resolution and exports
            MP4s shaped like the monitor's face, margins and scale included.
 - 0.9.12.1 Cameras shade faces like vanilla, draw the real font on signs
            and nametags, and no longer clip rotated signs at block edges.
 - 0.9.12.0 Microphone Link joins microphones to modems the same way cameras
            link; cameras now see other cameras' full posed models.
 - 0.9.11.0 Camera Link: join a camera to a wired modem through solid walls,
            no cables — six per modem, with the route shown while linking.
 - 0.9.10.0 Security camera gets its real model: pedestal base, swiveling
            post and tilting head, posed live on every mount.
 - 0.9.9.1  Scroll wheel zooms the hand-aiming scope.
 - 0.9.9.0  Cameras see everything: boats and minecarts, sign text, chests
            that stand open while in use, beds, banners, skulls and bells.
 - 0.9.8.0  Cameras render at full quality on dedicated servers: blocks bake
            from Mojang's assets, entities and player skins render for real.
 - 0.9.7.1  Monitors seen through a camera show their clean powered-off
            screens instead of texture noise.
 - 0.9.7.0  Microphones hear in stereo: voices pan across the sound stage by
            position, with left and right channels in the audio event.
 - 0.9.6.1  Exports no longer invert tones: recorded palettes store in the
            terminal's reversed slot order and readers now respect it.
 - 0.9.6.0  Capture card exports MP4 video instead of GIF: exact colors on
            every frame, real playback speed, and much smaller files.
 - 0.9.5.3  Exports share one steady palette for the whole clip and the
            camera smooths its exposure over time: no more color flashing.
 - 0.9.5.2  Busy dithered cells pick their two best palette shades instead of
            collapsing to extremes; desktop microphone model revised.
 - 0.9.5.1  Capture card GIF exports keep every frame's exact palette instead
            of guessing colors, and no longer refuse long recordings.
 - 0.9.5.0  Desktop microphone gets its real model and texture; voices fade
            with distance from the microphone instead of cutting off.
 - 0.9.4.0  Cameras report the players they can see as camera_player events,
            so programs can archive sightings while a loop buffer rolls.
 - 0.9.3.0  Recordings store at one standard size and playback rescales to
            fit the monitor, so any tape plays full-screen on any screen.
 - 0.9.2.0  Cameras film in black and white with CCTV auto-exposure and
            distance filtering; setColorMode picks sepia or color.
 - 0.9.1.0  Cameras mount on walls, floors and ceilings with a head that pans
            and tilts live, aimed from Lua or a right-click spyglass scope.
 - 0.9.0.0  Playback deck complete: the real model set with LED states, the
            loaded cassette visible with spinning reels, latching buttons.
